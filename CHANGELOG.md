## [0.1.1] - 2026-11-18

### Fixed

- The welcome window could open hidden behind other windows when Gazeling was opened from System Settings ("Open Anyway").

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-18

First public release.

### Added

- Short breaks (default: every 20 minutes for 20 seconds) and long breaks (default: every hour for 5 minutes), with settings to change both.
- Nine eye exercises, each animated by an owl and read out loud. Any exercise can be turned off, except the last remaining one.
- Heads-up notification before each break, with postpone and skip from the menu bar.
- Pause for a set time or until you resume.
- Multi-screen support: all screens dim, the owl appears on the screen you choose (including "The laptop screen"), and clicks pass through the dimmed screens.
- Time away from the computer counts as a break.
- Break chimes, which can be turned off.
- First-run setup for notifications and launch at login.
- Settings window, keyboard support and accessibility labels.
- "About Gazeling" window.
- App icon and menu bar icon.
